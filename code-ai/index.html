What a specialist is, and is not

Every CODE·A.I. specialist is advisory. It is a piece of software with a character, not a licensed practitioner, and it holds no qualification of any kind. Nothing it says is medical, veterinary, legal, financial, tax or mental-health advice, and no conversation with it creates a professional relationship. Names like Healer, Dietician, Veterinarian, Counselor, Jurist or Accountant describe the trade a character speaks about — they do not claim licensure in it. Decisions with real consequences deserve a real professional, and a good specialist will tell you so itself.

In an emergency, contact your local emergency services. This software is not a crisis service and cannot summon help.

What you pay, and what you pay separately

Chatterbot 2000 is free. A specialist is $9.99 per month, per specialist, billed automatically until you cancel. The model is not included: you bring your own API key from a provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic or DeepSeek and pay them directly for what you use, or point the app at a local model and pay nobody. We never see that key.

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record for every purchase. They take the payment, handle sales tax, and are where a subscription is cancelled — the receipt email carries the link.

Refunds and cancelling

Not happy, for any reason? Email within 14 days of your first payment and you get your money back — no questions and no form. After that, cancel any time and keep the month you have paid for. Renewals after the first fourteen days are not refunded.

A cancellation revokes nothing. Your key keeps working until the date you have paid through, and when that date passes the specialist stops and Chatty comes back. Nothing else in the app changes and nothing of yours is deleted.

Your data

There is no account and no sign-up. Your conversations, your files and your vault stay on your machine; what you ask goes to the model provider whose key you supplied, under their terms, and never to us. The one thing the app sends us is an installation ID at purchase, so the key can be delivered to the right copy.
