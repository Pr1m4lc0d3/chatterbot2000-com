What a specialist is, and is not

Every CODE·A.I. specialist is advisory. It is software with a character, not a licensed practitioner, and it holds no qualification, licence or registration of any kind.

Nothing a specialist says is medical, veterinary, legal, financial, tax or mental-health advice, and using one creates no professional relationship.

Names such as Healer, Apothecary, Dietician, Veterinarian, Counselor, Chaplain, Jurist, Accountant, Investor and Economist describe the trade the character speaks about. They do not claim licensure in it.

Decisions with real consequences — your health, an animal's health, your money, your legal position — deserve a qualified human being. Take what a specialist says as a well-read second opinion to bring to that person, never as a substitute for them.

In an emergency, contact your local emergency services. This software is not a crisis service and cannot summon help.

Language models also state false things confidently, including about their own sources. Check anything that matters before acting on it.
